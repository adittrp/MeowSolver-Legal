# MeowSolver — Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 1, 2026

Adit Tripathi ("developer," "we," "us") built MeowSolver as a free application. This page informs users of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information for anyone who uses this app.

## Information We Collect

MeowSolver does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app runs entirely on your device. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising frameworks.

Specifically, MeowSolver does **not**:

- Collect your name, email address, or any contact information
- Track your location
- Use cookies or similar tracking technologies
- Contain third-party analytics or advertising SDKs
- Transmit any data from your device to external servers

All puzzle data you create within the app stays on your device and is not accessible to us.

## Third-Party Services

MeowSolver does not integrate any third-party services that collect data on our behalf.

## Children's Privacy

MeowSolver does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The app is safe for users of all ages.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
